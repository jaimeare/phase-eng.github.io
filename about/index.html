Who we are

About Phase Engineering

A California electrical engineering firm where the Professional Engineer who seals the report is the one who walked your plant.

Phase Engineering was founded to bring clarity, reliability, and real engineering expertise to electrical safety and system design. We started it after watching too many companies struggle with inconsistent studies, unclear reports, and confusing compliance requirements.

We provide arc flash studies, coordination studies, short circuit analysis, NFPA 70E consulting, and full electrical design for projects of all sizes — whether you are upgrading equipment, planning new construction, or improving electrical safety.

Good engineering should reduce risk, improve reliability, and give you confidence in your electrical system. That is the standard we bring to every project.

How we work differently

A Professional Engineer collects the data

Data collection is the major hurdle on nearly every arc flash project, and the most common place studies go wrong. Frequently the correct system information never gets gathered, because field work was handed to electricians who were never told what the engineer needs.

Phase Engineering always sends a P.E. Electrical Engineer to gather data, with an electrician as support to open panel covers. The engineer who has to defend the model gathers its inputs.
