Multi-site programs

PG&E — 94 facilities

Arc flash studies across 94 facilities with panel counts ranging from 20 to 300 each, completed over two years as a sub-project of a large generator resiliency electrical design program.

The sites were divided into six groups served by six different electrical contractors, spread across California. Because return visits would have been impractical, the data collection effort had to be thorough the first time — which meant briefing each contractor on precisely what information was needed and reviewing everything that came back. The system model was then built from a combination of site data, as-builts, and shop drawings.

Short circuit, coordination, and arc flash calculations were developed, with equipment evaluation, coordination recommendations, and arc flash mitigation recommendations documented and walked through with the customer. After approval, labels were printed and installed on the equipment.

Electrical safety training

LAX — arc flash and electrical safety training

Six training sessions delivered to 30 students. Eight-hour sessions for employees with no prior arc flash training and four-hour refreshers for annual requalification. Content covered NFPA 70E, proper selection of PPE, proper installation of PPE, and hands-on lockout/tagout procedures.

James Hardie — Zero Harm training

Training delivered to 500 students in electrical safety and arc flash awareness, coordinated with the James Hardie safety department. Content drew on NFPA 70E alongside site-specific electrical safety policies.
